## Design and Advanced settings (per service)

You set these on each service under the Design and Advanced tabs. Do the "now" ones, the rest are optional.

**Design tab**
- Cover image and Thumbnail (do now): add a simple branded image so the service does not look bare. Cover 1280x780, thumbnail 600x600. Use your headshot or an SDR-themed graphic from your brand assets. Quick credibility lift.
- Showcase testimonials (do now): you have 8 testimonials and a 5/5 rating, but none are selected to display. Pick your best 2 or 3 for each service. This is the social proof that converts.
- Payment button label (optional): you can change "Book" to something like "Book my ramp session." Minor.
- Service label (optional): add a badge like Popular or Bestseller, but only on one or two services so it stays meaningful.
- Slash pricing (use honestly or skip): shows a struck-through higher price next to your real one. Only use it if there is a genuine discount, do not inflate a fake original, that breaks your no-overhyping rule.
- Sell session recording (optional): lets buyers re-watch the session. Nice value add, just be comfortable being recorded.

**Advanced tab**
- Country based pricing (review now, it is already ON): this is the answer to your INR vs USD question. Topmate charges each buyer based on their country's purchasing power, so set your INR base and it adjusts upward for the US and similar markets automatically. Click See prices once to sanity check what it is auto-setting, and Edit Pricing if any global number looks too low.
- Buffer time between calls (do now): set to 10 mins instead of 0, so back-to-back bookings do not burn you out.
- Custom booking confirmation message (do now, easy win): paste the message below.
- Discount codes (optional, nice for community): create one for SDR Leaders of APAC members or a launch offer.
- Redirect URL (later): once your website or newsletter is live, send buyers there after booking. Skip for now.
- Schedule for this service (leave Default): only change if a service needs special hours.
