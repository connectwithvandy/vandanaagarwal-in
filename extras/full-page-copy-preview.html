FAQ

Good questions before we start

Where should I start?
For personalized mentorship, start with the discovery call. Choose the AI working session if you want help with one specific SDR task. If you’re a founder setting up your SDR function, choose the SDR & GTM Blueprint consultation.
What happens on the mentorship discovery call?
We’ll discuss your goals, where you are right now, and what you need help with. That conversation helps me shape a personalized mentorship program and the next steps for working together.
What is an AI workflow?
A repeatable set of steps that uses AI to help with a task, such as turning account research into a relevant first outreach draft. We’ll work on one task and check the output together so you understand how to use it.
Do I need coding experience for the AI session?
No. We’ll start with your experience level, choose a manageable task, and explain each step as we build.
How is the AI session different from mentorship?
The AI session focuses on one practical task. Mentorship offers broader guidance around your SDR skills, challenges, confidence, and career direction.
Can you help me set up an SDR function from scratch?
Yes. We can work through your target customers, outbound approach, SDR setup, and go-to-market priorities, then map what to build first into a practical blueprint.
What do you mean by GTM and AI engineering?
GTM means go-to-market: how your company reaches and wins customers. GTM and AI engineering connect the tools, data, automation, and AI workflows that support that work. In the consultation, we’ll discuss where these fit in your SDR function and what to prioritize.
Does the founder consultation include building the entire system?
The consultation focuses on strategy, decisions, and a blueprint for your next steps. Any hands-on implementation would need a separately agreed scope.
Can we work together if I’m outside APAC?
Yes. I’ve built and scaled SDR teams across APAC, EMEA, and AMER. We’ll tailor the guidance to your market and goals.
